# Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-05

What is stored: the email address you sign up with, your display name, and whatever you or an AI assistant acting for you enter: people, organizations, openings, applications, notes, and documents you upload.

Invite requests: if you ask for an invite on talliard.com, the email address you leave is stored, with your answers to the optional questions after it, and is used only to send your invite and to decide who is invited first.

Who can see it: you, and any AI assistant you connect to your account. We can access the database to operate and support the service, and will not read your notes or documents except to fix a problem you report. Your data is not sold, and is not shared with third parties except the providers that host and run the service.

Where it lives: with reputable cloud providers, which may store and process it outside your country.

Research: as a beta user your data may be used for aggregate research into what works in a job search, for example reply rates by outreach channel, or how resume length relates to interview rates. This is done by automated analysis. Results never include quotes from your notes or documents, never identify you, a contact or an employer, and are only ever presented as totals across users.

Usage logging: the service records which screens and tools are used and when, to operate and improve it.

Session recording: during the beta, sessions in the app may be recorded and watched to fix what trips people up. The recordings show the app's own words, such as buttons, headings and error messages, and hide your records and anything you type.

Error reports: when something fails, the service sends a report of the failure, with your account id and the screen or tool involved but not what you wrote, to the provider that tracks errors.

Deletion: ask and your account and data are deleted within 30 days. Backups expire on their own schedule after that.
